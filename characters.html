Characters

Kiryu Kazuma

Also known as: Joryu, Taichi Suzuki, The Dragon Of Dojima. Main protagonist of the Yakuza/Like a Dragon franchise, also a legendary yakuza member. Currently assosciated with the Daidoji Faction. Former fourth chairman of the Tojo Clan. Former manager of the Morning Glory Orphanage located in Okinawa. Former taxi driver for Nagasu Taxi, former manager of Four Shine Sotenbori. Kazuma appeared in every mainline franchise game. Raised by Shintaro Kazama (of the Dojima Family), since Kiryu was an orphan himself. The man has a tattoo of an "Oryu/Yinglong" on his back, which is a symbol of power, wisdom, protection and balance in Japanese culture.

Haruka Sawamura

An adopted daughter of Kiryu Kazuma, whom she first meets in 2005. Wife of Yuta Usami and mother of Haruto Sawamura. Former idol, resident of the Morning Glory Orphanage in Okinawa, along with eight other children Kiryu has taken care of. Acts as an elder sister. Daughter of Yumi Sawamura - Kazuma's true love - who tragically died.

Goro Majima

Also known as "The Mad Dog of Shimano". Majima is one of the original characters, which occured in every single mainline game since the release of the first volume. Former captain of the Shimano Family. Former patriarch of the Majima Family. Founder and former president of the Majima Construction. Former vice-chairman of the Tojo Clan. The Mad Dog appeared a few times as the main protagonist. His fate is somewhat connected to Kiryu Kazuma. Majima is missing his left eye. He has a large tattoo, which covers his biceps, upper chest, stretching from his neck to his thighs. The tattoo consists of a floral motif with a Hannya, also with two snakes wrapped around his arms and chest. The Hannya mask is commonly believed to depict women who have turned into demons as a result of being consumed by rage, envy or sorrow. Snakes are often a symbol of rebirth and transformation due to their tendency to shed their skin.

Kaoru Sayama

Popularly known as the Yakuza Huntress. Lead detective of the Osaka Prefectural Police's Sotenbori District Organized Crime Division. Studied programming and cybersecurity, hence joining the tech crimes unit at the age of 20. Sayama has a very deep and complicated background. Throughout the most important task of her career, she came to known her actual descendancy and family story. (Un)knowingly connected to Ryuji Goda. Love interest of Kiryu Kazuma.

Ryuji Goda

Also known as the "Dragon of Kansai", which sparked some controversy in contrary with Kazuma's "Dragon of Dojima". Ryuji believed only one dragons belongs in Japan. Second patriarch of the Go-Ryu Clan, an Omi Alliance subsidiary. Famous for his strength and height, as well as his blonde hair and huge scar on his face. (Un)familiar with his actual descendancy and family story. His tattoo is the legendary gold dragon, Huang Long, who lords over the four gods of the cardinal directions, representing Ryuji's ambitions to rule over all of Japan's criminal underworld.

Rikiya Shimabukuro

Also known as "The Fighting Viper (of Okinawa)". Rikiya is the captain of the Ryudo Family. A very strong character with an original appearence. Has a punch perm hairstyle. Extremely loyal and connected to the place of his birth, hence why he has never been to the motherland. Met Kiryu in 2009 and became great friends ever since. His tattoo depicts a viper - specifically an Okinawan habu - pit viper native to the region.

Yoshitaka Mine

Main antagonist of Yakuza 3. Former lieutanant and interim chairman of the Tojo Clan, former chairman of the Hakuho Clan, former captain of the Nishikiyama Family, and the founder and former chairman on an unnamed startup company. An important element of the Tojo Clan's income between 2007 and 2009, gaining him the nickname of the "Safekeeper of the Tojo Clan". A close person to Daigo Dojima. Mine has a tattoo of Kirin - a mythical creature said to appear only in the presence of a leader and become protective if a pure person is threatened by a malicious one.

Shun Akiyama

Also known as "The Lifeline of Kamurocho". His net worth is believed to be over ¥100 billion. He is the CEO of the Sky Finance, working alongside his secretary Hana. He was a top employee in the Touto Bank, but got sacked due to the charges of embezzlement. Unfairly accused, he soon became homeless. However, due to the bomb explosion in the Millennium Tower, followed by the ¥10 billion raining down from the sky, Akiyama managed to gather some of the money to try his luck for the second time, with success.

Masayoshi Tanimura

Fairly called "The Parasite Of Kamurocho" due to his poor attitude towards work and open engagement in acts of corruption. However, he is always willingly open to help those in need. A Community Safety Division officer for the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department. Due to his upbringing of mixed descendancy, the man was helping the immigrant children who were left without parents. Masayoshi was even gambling and blackmailing illegal/criminal businesses to help the children in need.

Taiga Saejima

Due to his (in)famous slaying of the eighteen members of the Ueno Seiwa Clan and surviving to receive the death penalty, Saejima earned the nickname "18-Counts Saejima". Former patriarch of the Saejima Family and a former lieutenant of the Tojo Clan. A great friend of Goro Majima. Has a younger step-sister, Yasuko Saejima. His tattoo features a roaring tiger surrounded by bamboo grass. In Buddhism, it is believed that tiger and dragon are equal to each other, hence why Kiryu and Saejima are one of the most powerful yakuza members, yet having opposite traits.

Tatsuo Shinada

Retired batter for the Nagoya Wyverns, currently a nightlife journalist. Shinada was unfairly arrested on suspicion of game fixing and illegal gambling. While he was eventually cleared of all the charges, he was banned for life from professional baseball. He attended Kamuro West High School alongside Daigo Dojima. A man with a complicated story, living in poor conditions. However, he seems to enjoy his life.

Daigo Dojima

Once a drunk street thug, Daigo becomes the sixth (and the last) chairman of the Tojo Clan by the recommendation of Kazuma Kiryu. The son of Sohei Dojima represents a new generation of the yakuza. A charismatic and caring character, born to be a leader. Daigo has a tattoo of Fudo Myoo - defender of the Buddhist faith. Represents knowledge and power, thanks to the Kurikara sword. The fire represents the cleansing, while the dragon that surrounds the sword could be a sign of Kiryu's guidance for Daigo over the years.

Ichiban Kasuga

Ichiban became the main protagonist since Yakuza: Like a Dragon. Also known as "Hero of Yokohama". Former member of the Arakawa Family, subsidiary of the Tojo Clan. Former employee at Hello Work. A key member of the Great Dissolution of the Tojo Clan and the Omi Alliance. His tattoo covers most of his back and features a dragonfish - the in-between state of a koi fish transforming into a dragon. The legend of Koi states that the koi fish that swims up to the top of the waterfall is rewarded for its resilience by being transformed into a dragon. It is supposed to be a symbol of power, resilience and strength in the times of struggle and trouble.

Takayuki Yagami

Main protagonist of the spin-off series Judgment. Former defense attorney at Genda Law Office, currently serving as the director of Yagami Detective Agency, where he works as a private investigator alongside Masaharu Kaito. Yagami became a lawyer due to the fact that his own father was one. However, he felt the urge to follow his father under the circumstances of his elders decisions. His father was defending a man accused of strangling an underage girl, who he has proven not guilty. The consequences were real, as the victim's father murdered Yagami's parents and himself. This motivated Yagami to become a lawyer himself and "fix" the unfair reputation. The orphan had a successful career, which led to a similiar situation as in the past. Due to the murder done by his client, Yagami stepped down due to the feeling of guilt. He has started his own private investigative agency, Yagami Detective Agency.

Masaharu Kaito

A fellow friend and co-worker of Takayuki Yagami. Former lietuenant of the Matsugane Family. Former manager of Charles. Private investigator at Yagami Detective Agency. Due to a robbery in the family's office, he was almost forced to perform Yubitsume, which was disallowed by Yagami and Matsugane himself. Instead, he was expelled from the family. Shortly after, he has joined the Yagami Detective Agency, becomming a private investigator himself.

Tatsuya Ukyo

Major character in the spin-off series Kurohyou. Formerly a rowdly youth, gets involved with the Tojo Clan as a top fighter in their underground coliseum, earning him the nickname "The Mad Dragon of Kamuro".

Makoto Daito

Protagonist of STRANGER THAN HEAVEN. Born to an american father and japanese mother.
