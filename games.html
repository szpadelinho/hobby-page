Mainline

Yakuza [2005]

The debut release of SEGA's Toshihiro Nagoshi. Based in a 1995/2005 Tokyo's fictional entertainment district Kamurocho, we follow the story of young Kiryu Kazuma, who seeks for answers to many mysteries and tragedies. After serving ten years in prison, the man needs to find himself in a changed world of yakuza.

Yakuza 2 [2006]

A year after the emotional events of the previous installment, The Dragon of Dojima has to face new challenges, as the great danger arises from Osaka's Omi Alliance. With the help of Daigo Dojima and a later addition of Kaoru Sayama, they face new threats and enemies, such as the Jingweon Mafia. Includes a lot of twists and shocking discoveries.

Yakuza 3 [2009]

Following the events of 2006, Kazuma comes to a conclusion to leave Kamurocho with his adoptive daugher, Haruka. They leave Tokyo for Okinawa, where they open up an orphanage, Morning Glory. Thrown into a dirty business, the former yakuza must help his friend - Daigo Dojima - to stop Yoshitaka Mine from tragic consequences of his actions.

Yakuza 4 [2010]

A soft reset in the timeline happens with the introduction of three new protagonists: Shun Akiyama, Masayoshi Tanimura and Taiga Saejima. Four unique personalities with a lot in common bound together to fight with corrupt and disheartening opponents. Can a washed-up yakuza, money launderer, local detective and prison escapee save Kamurocho?

Yakuza 5 [2012]

With the addition of Tatsuo Shinada, we receive a much deeper and complex story of five protagonsits: Tatsuo Shinada, Kiryu Kazuma, Shun Akiyama, Taiga Saejima and Haruka Sawamura. Possibly one of the most well-written entries on the list. A retired baseballer, former yakuza, money launderer, prisoner and an idol - will everyone overcome their biggest struggles and achieve their dream?

Yakuza 0 [2015]

The series' prequel set in 1988 Kamurocho and Sotenbori, following the story of two protagonists: Kiryu Kazuma and Goro Majima. After being framed with murder, Kiryu leaves the Dojima Family in search of the answer for the complicated story of the Empty Lot. The one-eyed assassin however tries to repair the damage he has done in the past. He receives a chance to fix his mistakes, but everything changes once he meets the victim.

Yakuza Kiwami [2016]

A remake of the 2005 release of Yakuza, featuring a whole new game engine and refreshed arrangement. With the addition of a huge amount of mechanics, the game receives a whole new life to itself.

Yakuza 6 [2016]

A personal story of Kiryu Kazuma, who seeks justice for his adoptive daughter - Haruka Sawamura - who was a victim of a dramatic car accident. With shocking discoveries, the Dragon of Dojima has to face unexpected challenges and twists, including the discovery of a government secret, which consequences would be irreverseble.

Yakuza Kiwami 2 [2017]

After the success of the previous remake, the decision has been made to release a refreshed version of the 2006 Yakuza 2. However, this time RGG has decided to "play it safe" with minor and soft changes in contrary with the first Kiwami. Built on the recently introduced Dragon Engine.

Yakuza: Like a Dragon [2020]

A soft reboot of the series, with a new protagonist Ichiban Kasuga and a new gameplay style based on the J-RPG (turn-based roleplay) genre. The story follows a young man - Ichiban Kasuga - born and raised in a soapland in Kamurocho. After his life was saved by Masumi Arakawa, a patriarch of the Arakawa Family, young man decides to dedicate his life to serve his "hero". After being asked to to take the fall for Jo Sawashiro - family's captain - Kasuga comes to a conclusion to repay his debt. After serving eighteen years in prison, he faces confusing changes in the world - including the Omi Alliance control of Tokyos' Kamurocho. With new friends on his road, Ichiban has to find the reason of all his troubles.

Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth [2024]

A sequel of Yakuza: Like a Dragon with dual protagonists: Kiryu Kazuma and Ichiban Kasuga. With Ichiban seeking his mother, he travels to Honolulu, where he meets Kazuma. With similiar, yet different objectives, they connect their powers and help each other out. The grand finale consists of Kasuga fighting inequality and a great danger, while a terminally ill Kiryu stands face-to-face with his past. Probably the largest storyline in the series.

Yakuza Kiwami 3 [2026]

A controversial remake of the 2009 Yakuza 3, which received a modern engine with it's revelations. The game received mixed-to-negative opinions due to a massive change in the storyline, including a few recasts. The game came out with an additional "mini-game", Dark Ties.

Dark Ties [2026]

A bonus game included with Yakuza Kiwami 3, focusing on the main game's antagonist - Yoshitaka Mine - which is the main protagonist of this product. The release explains the backstory of the main villain, specifically before the main game's timeline. The finale connects with the main game's finale.

Spinoff

Ryu Ga Gotoku Kenzan! [2008]

First ever spin-off release from the Yakuza franchise. The game is set in historical Kyoto, Japan during the Edo period in 1605. The story follows the parts of Miyamoto Musashi's life. It is one of the two games based on historical figures and heritage of Japan. Kenzan has never been released outside of Japan, which makes it's status even more "iconic".

Kurohyou: Ryu Ga Gotoku Shinsho [2010]

An exclusive release for Sony PlayStation Portable. Just like the previous spin-off, Black Panther was never released outside of Japan. Produced by "Def Jam: Fight For NY"'s AKI Corporation, the game received a similiar gameplay style, focused more on one-on-one brawling, unlike the other Yakuza releases. The game features an original (in contrary with other franchise's releases) set of animated cutscenes.

Yakuza: Dead Souls [2011]

A Sony Play Station 3 exclusive release, who saw a global debut. Dead Souls takes place in a non-canon timeline after the events of Yakuza 4. It features a different style of gameplay - a third-person shooter. Four playable characters: Kiryu Kazuma, Ryuji Goda, Shun Akiyama and Goro Majima. The release follows the story of a zombie apocalypse that revolves around the protagonists trying to keep them and their loved ones safe while putting an end to this great danger.

Kurohyo 2: Ryu Ga Gotoku Ashura-hen [2012]

Sequel of the first Kurohyou game. Just like the previous installment, it is a PSP and Japan exclusive game. Includes the same exact unique features of the previous release.

Ryu Ga Gotoku Ishin! [2014]

A second release based on historical figures and stories. Japan exclusive, later released globally with the remake titled Like a Dragon: Ishin!. Ishin focuses on the Bakumatsu Period in Japan. The story focuses on the story of Sakamoto Ryoma, who seeks justice for the death of his mentor - Yoshida Toyo.

Judgment [2018]

A debut release of the successful spin-off series. Focusing on the life of a private detective, Takayuki Yagami, who tries to solve the mystery of the "the Mole" - a murderer and threat to Kamurocho. On his way to truth, Yagami finds shocking and disstressing discoveries.

Lost Judgment [2021]

The sequel to the successful debut of Judgment. This time, Yagami, tries to uncover the dark story of Seiryo High School. With tragic and visual discoveries, detective has to face the brutality of the world.

The Kaito Files [2022]

A downloadable content to Lost Judgment. This time, we follow the story of Masaharu Kaito - co-worker of Yagami - who searches for Mikiko Sadamoto alongside his alleged son, Jun Sadamoto.

Like a Dragon: Ishin! [2023]

A remake of the 2014 Ryu Ga Gotoku Ishin!. The game has been boosted with a few major gameplay changes, including multiple fighting styles, virtue system and special attributes.

Like a Dragon Gaiden: The Man Who Erased His Name [2023]

An interquel between Yakuza: Like a Dragon and Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth, focusing on the story of Joryu. The purpose of this release is to explain the further story and reasoning behind the "death" of Kiryu Kazuma. The game's finale connects with the finale of Yakuza: Like a Dragon. An emotional rollercoaster.

Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii [2025]

A spin-off focusing on the confusing story of Goro Majima, who suffers from amnesia. The game is somewhat connected to the story of the previous major release - Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth.

STRANGER THAN HEAVEN [2027]

An upcoming major release, serving as a possible prequel to the whole Yakuza / Like a Dragon series. Currently, we know that the game will feature five cities: Kokura (1915), Kure (1929), Minami (1943), Atami (1951), Shinjuku (1965). We follow the story of a half-Japanese boy named Makoto Daito. With the story spanning over 60 years of action, it seems to be the longest-paced game from the studio.
