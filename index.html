The Like a Dragon experience

This website is dedicated to one of my most favourite video game franchises of all time - Yakuza (originally called "Ryu Ga Gotoku" [Like a Dragon] in Japan). It is without a doubt a one of a kind collection of unique aesthetic and quite original storyline, including the gameplay style. It consists of 23 entries, including base products and remakes. Since the first release, which happened in the year 2005, we follow the story of a man - Kiryu Kazuma. He is the main protagonist of the whole series, who occurs in most of the games as the character we follow. The main timeline spans over 40 years. With that being said, the franchise points out the social and common problems in Japan due to the specific time period or cultural differations. The series became internationally successful after almost 10 years, with the release of Yakuza 0. It was a crucial point for the series, which allowed to "open" the series to the west, where previously some of the games have not even received an international release, with some of the reasons being the cultural difference and possible "cultural misunderstanding". The franchise itself became a huge "meme" itself, due to the humorous and absurd nature of some situations, including the cutscenes. Features a "beat-em-up" style of gameplay with a "sparkle" of RPG. However, since the release of the game Yakuza: Like a Dragon, the mainline gameplay style has changed to J-RPG, similiar to other franchises like Persona.

However, one of the most important elements of the series is the soundtrack itself. The production is heavily praised for the original approach and unique connection, creating a one of a kind atmopshere. With the help of legendary SEGA composer Hidenori Shoji and other notable composers, such as Mitsuharu Fukuyama, who created probably the biggest song in the franchise - Baka Mitai (eng. I was a fool). That concludes the fact, that the whole soundtrack in the franchise is original, apart from a few exceptions, where actual licensed songs were included in the game. Although most of these were only in the japanese versions of the release, it still should be a notable mention. Production ranges from genres like rock or metal, jazz, groove, ballads, enka, j-pop to even synthwave, disco and dubstep.

Through the series, we follow the stories of multiple protagonists. Even though every single person is unique, their problems are universal. Some of the stories hold universal truths or morals. Most notably, the series points out how self-destructive a human ego can be. At the same time, we can witness the strength of friendship, which can sound childish, yet realistically true.

Some of the morals include traditional values, like family, which is probably the most important symbol. With the main protagonist being an orphan, including his future adoptive daughter, we realize what it means to be a family and what it takes to create one. This is also factual due to the fact Kiryu had his own orphanage in Okinawa. Although, with organisations like the Tojo Clan we also come to a realization how important respect is in our daily life.
